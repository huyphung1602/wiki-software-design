# Overview

By John Ousterhout, Stanford University. 2nd Edition, 2021 (Yaknyam Press). 210 pages.

A short, opinionated book arguing that the primary goal of software design is to reduce [[complexity]]. Based on Ousterhout's experience teaching CS 190 at Stanford, where students learn design through iterative code reviews. Every principle comes from real coding experience.

## Core Themes
- [[complexity]] is the central enemy — design is about minimizing it
- [[problem-decomposition]] is the fundamental design skill
- [[strategic-programming|Strategic programming]] — invest in design upfront
- Deep modules, information hiding, and simplicity over cleverness
