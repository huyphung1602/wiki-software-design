8.5 Extensions
The relational algebra — whilst flexible — is a restrictive language in com-
putational terms (it is not Turing-complete) and is normally augmented in
various ways when used in practice. Common extensions include:
General computation capabilities for example simple arithmetical op-
erations, possibly along with user-defined computations.
Aggregate operators such as MAX, MIN, COUNT, SUM, etc.
Grouping and Summarization capabilities to allow for easy applica-
tion of aggregate operations to relations
Renaming capabilities theabilitytogeneratederivedrelationsbychang-
ing attribute names
